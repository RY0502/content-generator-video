## Concept

Every night after children fall asleep, their toys secretly come alive.

While the children sleep, the toys explore the house, solve small problems, help forgotten toys, and complete secret missions before morning.

The world should feel magical from the toys' tiny perspective.

A bedroom becomes a huge world of furniture, blankets, drawers, shelves, and toys.

## Main Characters

- **Toby the Teddy Bear** — kind, brave, and the natural leader.
- **Pip the Toy Robot** — clever and loves building or fixing things.
- **Lulu the Doll** — caring, imaginative, and good at understanding feelings.
- **Zoom the Toy Car** — energetic, funny, and extremely fast.
- **Buttons the Plush Bunny** — small, nervous, but surprisingly courageous.
- **Mr. Tick-Tock** — an old wind-up toy who knows the secrets of the house.

The toys should have distinct personalities and solve problems by combining their different abilities.

## Story Rules

- Adventures happen at night while the child sleeps.
- Everyday household objects should feel enormous and exciting.
- Problems should be gentle and relatable.
- Focus on friendship, kindness, teamwork, courage, and helping others.
- The toys must return to their places before morning.
- Occasionally include the suspense of hearing the child move or waking up, but keep it playful rather than scary.

## Sample Episode Ideas

### The Missing Sock
A tiny toy sock disappears before a doll's bedtime party. The toys search under the bed, behind furniture, and inside the laundry basket, discovering that the "missing sock" has become a cozy sleeping bag for a tiny toy mouse.

### The Broken Kite
A toy kite brought home from the park has a broken tail. The toys secretly repair it using ribbon, string, and craft supplies before morning.
